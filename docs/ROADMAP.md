# Roadmap

The aim: the best private family archive, where AI helps but does not invent, and the result can be given to relatives. World archives, DNA and offline sync are out of scope.

Done: see [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

Next:

- **Family book.** One good template: cover, contents, chapters by branch, a page per person with photos and biography, a chart, an index of names.
- **A standalone family site.** A static copy to share, with everything private physically left out before packing.
- **Transcribing voices,** on the owner's own key, with the original kept alongside and nothing entering the record without confirmation.

Ideas and needs are welcome in the [issues](https://github.com/danarkhipov/folkbranch/issues).
